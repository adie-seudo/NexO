RA 10173 • Data Privacy Notice

Data Privacy Notice & Protection Policy

This Data Privacy Notice outlines how NexO collects, processes, and safeguards institutional student records, verified academic documents, and provider information in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012 of the Philippines).

01

Collection of Institutional .edu.ph Email Addresses

Student eligibility and authentication protocol.

To uphold the credibility and fairness of the scholarship distribution process, NexO mandates that all student user registrations utilize an active institutional email address ending with the .edu.ph domain suffix (e.g., student@g.batstate-u.edu.ph).

This mandatory requirement serves the following verified purposes:

  • Enrolled Identity Verification: Confirms that every applicant is an active, enrolled collegiate student within an accredited Philippine higher education institution.
  • Prevention of Sybil & Duplicate Accounts: Eliminates illegitimate, bot, or duplicate student accounts, guaranteeing that each student holds only one verified application profile.
  • Official Academic Correspondence: Guarantees that sensitive status notifications, document verification alerts, and award decisions reach the applicant's official student inbox.

Institutional email addresses are strictly validated during registration and cannot be modified to non-academic domains.

02

Secure Academic Document Handling via Digital Drop-Box

Upload quarantine, file validation, and restricted reviewer access.

When submitting a scholarship application, students transmit verified academic proofs through the Digital Drop-Box. These documents typically include:

  • Certificates of Registration (COR) and Enrollment Proofs
  • Official Grade Slips and Transcripts of Records (TOR)
  • Barangay Certificates of Indigency or Income Documentation
  • Valid Government or University-Issued Student Identification Cards

The NexO backend enforces stringent security controls across the upload pipeline:

  • Strict MIME Type Validation: Only validated document formats (application/pdf, image/jpeg, image/png) are accepted. Executable scripts, binaries, and unverified archive files are automatically rejected.
  • Per-File Capacity Limiter: Document file sizes are restricted to an explicit 10MB quota per document slot to prevent server denial of service.
  • Isolated Server Storage: Uploaded attachments are quarantined within designated local server storage directories (/uploads/) using unique timestamped file signatures to avoid file collision or overwrite.
  • Restricted Reviewer Scoping: Document files are accessible solely to the authorized scholarship provider evaluating the specific grant applied for. No student document is exposed in public directory feeds.
03

Local Data Storage Protocols (Zero External Cloud Transmission)

Host-level isolation with zero third-party commercial data exposure.

A foundational security tenet of NexO is its Zero Cloud / Zero Commercial Third-Party Dependency architecture. All system state, applicant submissions, provider settings, and grant listings are stored and processed strictly within the local host server environment via atomic JSON data files.

Privacy Assurance: Your personal data, contact number, institutional email, and uploaded academic records are NEVER transmitted to external commercial databases, public cloud storage, advertising aggregators, or analytical tracking networks.

All file writes are performed atomically on the server host filesystem, ensuring transaction integrity without risking remote data leaks.

04

Student Rights Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act

Exercising data subject rights on the NexO platform.

As a student data subject, you are entitled to statutory rights under RA 10173, including:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know how your academic information is processed and reviewed before submitting any application.
  • Right of Access: You can review your submitted applications, uploaded file names, and provider decision statuses anytime via the Application Tracking Board and View My Submissions console.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update and correct your contact number, university, degree program, and year level anytime through your Student Settings page.
  • Right to Object & Withdraw: You may choose not to apply for specific grants or contact the system administrator to request full application removal prior to provider evaluation.
05

Provider Data Protection Obligations & Inquiries

Confidentiality mandates and institutional contact.

Scholarship providers registered on NexO agree to treat all student applicant submissions with the highest degree of confidentiality. Providers are strictly prohibited from redistributing, commercializing, or using student documents for any purpose outside evaluating eligibility for their specific published scholarship grant.
