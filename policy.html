Data Privacy Principles (RA 10173)

Student verification and local document security.

Our privacy protocol is built around institutional student trust:

  • Mandatory .edu.ph Email: Validates collegiate scholar status and prevents fraudulent duplicate accounts.
  • Digital Drop-Box Isolation: PDF, JPG, and PNG documents are stored strictly in quarantined local directories.
  • Zero External Cloud Tracking: No personal or academic records are ever shared with third-party advertising or analytics networks.

Terms of Service Summary

Single application rules and provider listing standards.

Key operating rules for all users:

  • Single-Application Constraint: Each student may apply only once per scholarship grant post to ensure fair distribution.
  • Applicant Quota Limiting: Providers configure applicant limits (e.g. 200 applicants) with automated cutoff upon quota fulfillment.
  • Authenticity & Confidentiality: Students must submit authentic records, and providers must keep all submissions confidential.
