01

Academic Prototype Status & User Acceptance

Operating framework and scope of the NexO platform.

By accessing or creating an account on NexO, you acknowledge and agree that NexO is an academic software solution developed for IT 314 Web Systems and Technologies under the College of Informatics and Computing Sciences (CICS) at Batangas State University - The National Engineering University, Alangilan Campus.

The platform is designed to emulate and operate a production-grade scholarship portal using local data handling protocols. You agree to use the platform in good faith, in adherence to university academic integrity standards.

02

Student Applicant Terms & The Single-Application Rule

Equitable opportunity and authentic document submission.

As a student applicant, you agree to the following mandatory conditions:

  • Institutional Registration: You must register using a verified .edu.ph university email address representing your real student identity.
  • Strict Single Application Rule: To preserve fairness and equal distribution of scholarship opportunities among collegiate peers, students are permitted to submit exactly one (1) active application per published scholarship grant. Duplicate submissions to the same scholarship post are strictly prohibited and automatically blocked by the system.
  • Authenticity of Uploaded Credentials: All academic transcripts, grade slips, certificates of registration, and identity documents uploaded to the Digital Drop-Box must be genuine, unaltered, and belonging to the registered applicant. Submitting falsified documents constitutes academic dishonesty and results in immediate disqualification.
  • Application Status Respect: Decisions rendered by scholarship providers (Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Rejected) are final for that specific application cycle.
03

Scholarship Provider Commitments & Listing Standards

Transparent guidelines, timely evaluation, and strict confidentiality.

Scholarship providers (LGUs, corporate foundations, alumni associations, and NGOs) agree to:

  • Listing Accuracy: Publish truthful descriptions, realistic deadlines, and clearly specified documentary requirements.
  • Applicant Quota Adherence: Specify a concrete Applicant Limit (e.g. 50, 100, 200 applicants) during grant creation. Once the applicant count matches the defined quota, the system will automatically mark the grant as Quota Full to prevent over-subscription and preserve review feasibility.
  • Nondiscriminatory & Fair Review: Review all eligible applicant dossiers objectively without discrimination based on gender, religion, or background, adhering strictly to stated merit or financial need criteria.
  • Absolute Confidentiality: Keep all reviewed student PDFs, grade sheets, contact details, and indigency proofs strictly confidential within the NexO review console.
04

Data Persistence & Local Storage Architecture

Zero external cloud dependency and atomic file transactions.

NexO operates entirely through local Node.js file system APIs and JSON data storage. No financial transactions, bank credentials, or credit card numbers are ever processed or requested on this platform.

Uploaded student files are quarantined locally within designated /uploads/ directories and served exclusively to authorized reviewers. Users may delete or update their profile information at any time through their respective Account Settings.
